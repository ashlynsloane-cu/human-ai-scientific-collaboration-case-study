# Phase 19 methods — provenance, sampling, and stopping gates

## New investigator clarifications

Three previously excluded conversations have sufficient direct project-identity evidence for inclusion: a December 2025 comparison of two Hsp60/10-interactor groups, and two computational research figure/manuscript workflows whose text incidentally contains the uppercase AREA token. These were identified **nonrandomly** and change corpus membership by three records. Previous AI/human ratings are not modified.

## Review workflow

The probability sample targets 60 of 351 remaining exclusions (uniform without replacement) and is accompanied by 10 included positive controls. The mixed 70-case list does not display previous ratings. Each case retains a source-evidence fingerprint, text excerpt, and context sufficiency field. A separate purposive seven-case queue documents potential eligibility-boundary ambiguity and is excluded from probability estimates.

After human ratings are returned, compute the frequency of INCLUDE among the **60 locked excluded cases**. Keep UNCERTAIN and missing ratings separately and report sensitivity analysis for unresolved cases. Only the 60 randomly selected exclusions support an estimate for the 351 remaining excluded conversations; the 10 controls and seven purposive cases do not.

The review is not independent of the investigator's historical project knowledge. If publication requires a stronger claim, have a nonparticipant coder re-evaluate a privacy-cleared subsample. Distinguish text-evidenced project membership from investigator recollection, and separately audit source texts that omit attachments.

## Stopping conditions

Do not freeze the corpus until the probability sample is human-reviewed, consequential reversals are adjudicated, error-rate uncertainty is quantified appropriately, and the two documented AREA false positives and post-freeze project-boundary amendments are retained in the protocol history.
