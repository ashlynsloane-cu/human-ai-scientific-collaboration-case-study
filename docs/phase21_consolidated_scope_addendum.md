# Phase 21 — Consolidated computational-project eligibility scope (post-freeze amendments)

This document **does not revise the frozen V1 protocol in place**. It consolidates investigator-directed scope amendments made during Phases 10–20 and is to be versioned as an eligibility-specific addendum if adopted. These conditions were not prespecified in the November 12, 2026 protocol freeze.

## Target population
The unit of eligibility is an exported ChatGPT **conversation**. Study frame: all 597 conversations in the supplied 11-shard export created at or after **2025-10-01 00:00 America/Denver**; the 452 older conversations are outside the frame (not substantively classified as irrelevant). The export cannot establish completeness of deleted chats, other AI platforms, Codex, or unavailable assets.

## Positive inclusion condition
Include a conversation that materially developed, evaluated, tested computationally, visualized, interpreted, wrote up, revised, or made reproducible the Hsp60/10–ROSMAP computational research project. This **includes exploratory and abandoned computational investigations** that helped develop the hypothesis, as well as analyses and manuscript work from December 2025/January 2026. Do not restrict to methods or outputs retained in the final manuscript. A mixed conversation is included when it contains qualifying computational-project work, irrespective of unrelated content elsewhere in the same thread.

## Explicit exclusions
Exclude independent fall 2025 and January–March 2026 **wet-lab experimental programs** even when they involve Hsp60/Hsp10/MIC, unless that conversation itself materially contributed to the computational-project reasoning. Exclude separate ROSMAP–AREA rotation-project work even when ROSMAP data/methods overlap. Exclude unrelated coursework, fitness, personal/administrative activity, and mentions of the paper that do not perform research work (e.g. fellowship narrative).

## Contextual AREA exception
The previously applied *literal* uppercase `AREA` filter was overbroad. An `AREA` occurrence in figure layout/code text or in an assistant response does not identify the separate research project. The Hsp60/10 conversations `Professional Adjustments Guide` and `Revision planning` were restored after investigator confirmation. Project linkage and full conversation context—not tokens in isolation—govern eligibility.

## Evidence hierarchy and provenance
Preserve immutable first-pass AI ratings, original blinded human decisions, and chronological append-only adjudication overlays. Investigator firsthand knowledge establishes project membership where documented; it is not independent validation. Source-text evidence outranks titles alone. A project-related conversation is not proof that a particular analysis was actually implemented. Do not calculate contribution scores at this stage.

## Sensitivity disclosures
Report the originally frozen broader inclusion concept, post-freeze date cutoff, unrelated wet-lab boundary, and AREA-rule correction in the methods. Explain that 245/352 are working decisions in an **export-defined** frame. The Phase 19 sample of 60 originally excluded cases produced zero confirmed false exclusions after adjudication, with an approximately 4.27% one-sided 95% finite-population upper bound for the original 351 exclusions, conditional on the sampling design and review labels. Do not claim zero error or account-wide exhaustiveness.
