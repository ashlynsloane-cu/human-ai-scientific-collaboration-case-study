# Gates before eligibility corpus freeze and impact analysis

1. **Finish completeness coverage:** source-context review of 408 remaining conversations, accounting for all 1,049 unique IDs with a versioned, non-overwriting decision lineage. Do not confuse retrieval scan with meaningful project eligibility adjudication.
2. **Resolve missing context:** prioritize uncertain cases with project-distinguishing experiment names, sample identifiers (without sharing restricted rows), named scripts/figures, and legitimate connections to the Hsp60/10 analysis history. Keep genuinely unresolvable records `uncertain` in the frozen corpus and report sensitivity analyses rather than forced labels.
3. **Human blinded safeguard:** prioritize the prepared but unfinished Phase 8 40-case sample and the Phase 6 80-case probability sample. Preserve initial AI labels and human labels before revealing discrepancies. Prepare privacy-cleared source context; avoid imposing multiple redundant review packets simultaneously.
4. **External reviewer:** if feasible, an uninvolved scientific coder independently reviews an appropriately redacted predefined subset. Expert investigator review and external independent agreement serve different validity functions.
5. **Multimodal/branch bias:** examine material unavailable via selected text excerpts, especially cases whose relevance depends on a figure, spreadsheet, or attached protocol. Record missing payload as an evidence limitation; never infer E0–E4 or absence of controlled-access E5 data.
6. **31-case baseline reconciliation:** match reconstructed candidate records to original exported conversations by date and scientific content, documenting ambiguous and one-to-many mappings instead of guessing by title.
7. **Measure disagreement transparently:** report first-pass AI/human agreement separately from post-discussion adjudication. Use a confusion matrix, percent agreement, kappa with confidence intervals where possible, and strata-specific analyses. Do not use the enriched Phase 9 selection as an unbiased prevalence sample.
8. **Corpus version freeze:** only after source traceability, coverage review, human validation and adjudication documentation, assign a separate versioned eligible-corpus index. Do not change the V1 intervention coding manual to make results easier to obtain.
9. **Only then** identify AI proposals, substantive human steering events, downstream implementation, artifact propagation, and retention—each using separate evidence, not conversational similarity alone.
