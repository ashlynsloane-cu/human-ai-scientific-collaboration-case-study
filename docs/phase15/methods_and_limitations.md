# Phase 15 methods and limits

The investigator confirmed that 22 targeted conversations concerning mitochondrial protein lists, enrichment/GSEA/Cytoscape, Seurat/bulk RNA-seq, and Hsp60/10 figure/analysis iterations belonged to the computational Hsp60/10 research project. Two separate experimental conversations did not.

Original Phase 14 labels and the original AI labels are immutable; Phase 15 decisions are saved as an overlay with conversation identifiers and evidence-source fingerprints **only in the private archive**. An earlier human UNCERTAIN decision for one case is similarly preserved rather than rewritten.

The resolution is based on investigator project knowledge, not adjudication against the AI's original classification. Project membership alone does not establish that a human intervention occurred or had downstream effect; these are later analyses. Note selection bias and retrospective-memory uncertainty.

An investigator decision is not equivalent to fully transcript-corroborated classification; the basis is separately recorded. The 77 unresolved cases require additional evidence or human review. A probability audit of exclusions remains necessary to assess false omissions.
