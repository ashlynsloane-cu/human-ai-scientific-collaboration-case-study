- **Decision date:** November 13, 2026.
- **New primary corpus start:** October 1, 2025, 00:00 America/Denver time (inclusive).
- **Decision provenance:** Investigator asserts that all Hsp60/10 project-relevant ChatGPT conversations occurred on or after this date, and explicitly directs that older chats not be reviewed further.
- **Relationship to frozen V1:** This scope restriction was declared after the protocol freeze and Phases 5–9. It is not presented as a prespecified rule, nor is it a silent modification to the frozen V1 file. The explicit amendment and any sensitivity implications must be documented before primary analysis.
- **Unit:** Whole conversation. Date comes from export creation metadata; source-message timestamps were checked for cross-boundary conversations. No conversations started before the cutoff and continued after it, avoiding partial-conversation reclassification.
- **Scope exclusion is NOT a substantive eligibility judgment:** 452 older conversations leave the *date-defined sampling frame*, not because they have been independently adjudicated as irrelevant. Their historical preliminary labels remain locked in their original sources.
- **Source status:** The resulting 597-conversation population is complete for the supplied ChatGPT export only. It is not a census of Codex, other AI systems, deleted conversations, or unexported branches.

- **Limits:** 206 include / 274 exclude / 117 uncertain are *provisional working labels*, not the final eligible corpus or a prevalence estimate. The Phase 10 tranche is a rule-assisted source-user-context first pass from one AI reviewer. Content and source pointers remain private.
- **Reliability consequences:** Of the original 40 blinded human eligibility cases, seven are now outside the revised date range. The originally reported 38/40 exact agreement and Cohen's kappa remain historical figures on the original sample and must not be labeled as post-cutoff validation without recalculating a suitable in-scope sample.
